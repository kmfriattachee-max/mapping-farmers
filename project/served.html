Overview

AquaMap Kenya is KMFRI's open aquaculture mapping platform — a lightweight GIS for registering farms, visualising water resources, and connecting farmers with hatcheries, markets and suppliers. The platform provides tools for county planning, farmer support, and simple analytics to inform decisions.

Platform Features

Farmer Registration

Register and manage fish farms.

GIS Mapping

Visualize farms and aquaculture resources.

Water Resource Analysis

Identify nearby rivers and water sources.

Buffer Analysis

Find farms within specified distances of rivers.

Market Intelligence

Locate fish markets and buyers.

Analytics Dashboard

Generate reports and statistics.

Production Monitoring

Track production trends.

Mobile Friendly

Accessible on phones, tablets and desktops.
